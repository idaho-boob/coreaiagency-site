Questions

The basics, in plain English.

What is an AI agent?

An AI agent is a digital team member that works for your business. It can answer your phone, reply to texts and web chats, follow up with leads and book appointments. It is not a real estate or insurance agent. It works from instructions and information about your business.

Can AI answer my business phone after hours?

Yes. An AI receptionist can answer calls 24/7, collect the caller's details, answer common questions and book appointments on your calendar. Urgent calls can be sent to your phone.

Will callers know they are talking to AI?

Modern AI voices sound natural, and we set the agent up around how your business talks. Callers can always ask for a person, and urgent or sensitive calls can be handed off to you.

What kinds of businesses use AI agents?

Contractors such as roofers, plumbers, electricians and HVAC companies, plus dentists, doctors, chiropractors, mortgage brokers, real estate agents, attorneys, coaches and solo operators. Any business that loses customers when nobody answers can use one.

How does setup work?

You talk with us once about your business. We build and connect the agent to your phone and calendar, you test it, and then it goes live. You get a short summary of every conversation.

Do you offer AI consulting and one-on-one AI training?

Yes. We help business owners figure out where AI saves time and money, and we teach you and your team hands-on, one-on-one, in person in the Treasure Valley or over Zoom.

Where are you located and who do you serve?

Core AI Agency is based in Meridian, Idaho. We work with businesses in the Boise and Treasure Valley area in person, and with businesses anywhere by phone and Zoom.
