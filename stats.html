What happens when nobody answers

These are published figures from call-tracking and research companies. We list where each one comes from so you can check it. Some come from companies that sell phone or answering tools, so treat them as strong indicators, not guarantees.

When nobody picks up

About 85% of callers hang up on voicemail without leaving a message.Quo analyzed 18M+ missed calls, Jul-Sep 2026: only about 15% left a voicemail (14% after hours). Source
27% of calls to home-services businesses go unanswered.Invoca, 2026. Source. Invoca's 2026 benchmark also found a person answered 52% of inbound home-services calls, ranging 32% to 74% by trade.
Fewer than 3% of callers sent to voicemail leave a message (Invoca platform data).Source
Only 37.8% of calls to small businesses were answered by a person.411 Locals, 85 businesses across 58 industries, 30 days (2016, small sample). 37.8% went to voicemail and 24.3% got no response.

What a missed call costs

$300+ in immediate revenue per missed home-services call, often much more for roofing and larger jobs.Industry estimates (Invoca figures cited by JobNimbus: $100-$300 typical). Boise-area jobs often run higher; this is a general guide, not a local measurement.
38% of calls went unanswered across a 26-practice dental group; new-patient calls converted at 25%.Peerlogic, 4,280 calls, Feb 2026. Source. Dental missed-call value is commonly estimated at $300+ for the first visit alone, with far more in lifetime value.

Speed wins the job

21x more likely to qualify a lead when you respond in 5 minutes instead of 30 (about 100x more likely to reach them).MIT / InsideSales.com Lead Response Management study, 15,000+ leads, 100,000+ call attempts. Measures reaching and qualifying leads, not closed sales; study is from 2007. Source
Almost 40% of homeowners rarely or never use a contractor who missed their first call; 64% said answering the first call matters.Service Direct survey of 559 US homeowners, 2022 (run by a lead-generation company).
78% of consumers have abandoned a business after an unanswered call; 21% immediately call another business.CallRail 2025 survey of 1,000 US consumers.
